01 · About

About me

I'm a CS student at Cal State Fullerton, and I think a lot about how products should work: who they're for, what they need to do first, and what makes someone want to come back. Then I build them, mostly with React, TypeScript, Flutter, and Python.

I like making things with some personality. Rey, my AI terminal assistant, comes with a pixel-art dog I designed frame by frame, and I built this site to open like a file folder.

I'm usually the one organizing the team. I led Coffee Tenet's mobile app from the first product brief to the build, ran sprints as a project management intern at SELECT, and helped lead a 400+ member organization at CSUF. I'm looking for product management and software engineering roles where I can lead a team and own both the planning and the building.
